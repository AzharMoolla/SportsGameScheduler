## Rebuild plan

Keep the left emblem and right action scene as separately rendered pieces. Share named material definitions, the basketball model and lighting conventions, not a connecting physical floor. Export each side independently so mobile framing and future camera changes do not require rebuilding a huge composition.

| Element | Blender construction and acceptance check |
| --- | --- |
| Left ball | High-resolution smooth sphere, accurately wrapped channels, restrained leather normal detail. Dark leather and controlled orange channel emission. Check silhouette and seam topology against the concept at final display size. |
| Left hoop | Circular metal rim with correct wall thickness, separate net attachment hooks and braided cords forming a tapered hanging net. No cords crossing through the rim. |
| Right basket | Glass backboard, centered target rectangle, rim projecting perpendicular from the center bottom edge, connected support arm and base. Align these geometrically before lighting. |
| Court | Regulation half-court proportions and markings derived from one coordinate system. Baseline, key, free-throw arc and three-point arc must relate to the basket; do not copy generated inaccuracies. |
| Finish | Smooth shaded beveled metal, glass transmission, controlled wood reflections, leather detail visible without gritty overamplification. Clean high-sample render with denoising. Orange glow accents with retained material detail, not clipped highlights. |
| Cameras | One fixed camera for each piece. Preserve full subject framing, coherent perspective and safe negative space. Check silhouettes before adding fine detail. |
| Animation | Design ball trajectory and net response after static geometry/material approval. A keyed rotation is not physical net simulation. |
| Light mode | Separate anime/ink/watercolor art direction. Blender may supply geometry, pose and linework, with painted pigment textures and illustration compositing. Do not reuse dark shading with a lighter background. A detailed athlete requires a suitable rigged mesh and careful pose/anatomy work. |

Generated targets are guides, not mechanically accurate blueprints. Check rim/backboard alignment, court geometry and athlete anatomy independently. Match framing and material quality before broadening to other sports. Do not claim parity until final-size browser comparison supports it.

No third-party models were downloaded or imported for these concepts. Model-library assets remain candidates subject to per-model license, texture and quality review.
